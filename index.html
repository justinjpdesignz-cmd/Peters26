Getting around

Golf carts are a major part of the transportation network at the resort. To request a cart, just dial 0 from your resort room phone and ask for a cart. The operator answers in Spanish, so just say “cart, please” and your location. You can also get picked up at a cart stop along the paths and restaurants.

The resort sits on a fairly steep incline, so carts are handy for uphill and downhill trips, but if you’re a walker there are tons of great walking paths!

All inclusive

Your all-inclusive plan covers the majority of items at the resort. You will see prices on the menus. These are for guests without the all-inclusive plan, and if an item requires a surcharge, it will be stated.

Two restaurants have a surcharge for dinner: the Steakhouse Restaurant at Quivira Golf Club ($40 USD) and LaFrida ($30 USD). Both also require a smart casual dress code.

Menus, dress codes, and current hours for all restaurants are in the Pueblo Bonito app.
Sister resorts

There is a free shuttle to the Pueblo Bonito sister resorts Rose and Los Cabos in downtown Cabo. Your all-inclusive benefits also work at these resorts if you want to try a different restaurant or just see more of beautiful Cabo. The beaches at these two resorts are also swimmable!

We highly suggest pre-booking the shuttle in the Pueblo Bonito app if you’re interested.
Activities

The resort has multiple daily activities, and the full list with locations and times is in the app. Examples include cornhole, beach and pool volleyball, water aerobics, Zumba, yoga, spin, and many more! Sunset Beach is family friendly, with a kids’ pool and kids’ activities by the pool. For older kids there’s even a “Fun Lab” with an arcade, movie theater, and more.

There are also wine and tequila tastings, an award-winning spa, and an award-winning golf course.

Feel free to enjoy outside activities in Cabo too, like ATV rides, boat tours, fishing trips, zip lining, and more. Cabo is also known for shopping, from tourist shops to luxury shops, and in San José del Cabo there is an art walk every Thursday evening.

Suite service

What’s better than room service on vacation? Not much! You can have a wide variety of items delivered straight to your suite. Check the app for details.

Suite service costs $5 per visit.
The Market

The Market is a popular place! It’s an authentic food hall with nine stations of different cuisines: Asian, Italian, Mexican, desserts, cocktails, and American-style diner. Popular picks: the coffee stand, crepe station, and gelato!

For a sneak peek, see their Instagram.

Theme nights at La Nao

At La Nao, by the main pool, there is a different themed buffet dinner every night, and a fabulous breakfast buffet each morning!

Check the Pueblo Bonito app for the La Nao theme night schedule.
Safety first

The resort has a medical center on property, and the doctor can also make “house” calls to your room. Hopefully not needed, but just in case!
